TON · Sign data

Sends ton_signData. After a signature, the dApp rebuilds the signed bytes and verifies them with ton_getPublicKey from the session.

TON · Send (1–4 messages)

One ton_sendMessage with every row as a message, valid_until = now + 300. A comment becomes a text payload BOC.

TON · Jetton transfer

One message to your jetton wallet with 0.05 TON attached and a transfer payload (op 0x0f8a7ea5, response to your address).

BSC · Sign

Signatures are checked locally (verifyMessage / verifyTypedData) against the connected address. Permit uses the token, spender and amount from the token panel, deadline = now + 3600.

Break the request

Applied to every request the TON / BSC panels above send, until unchecked (not the raw panel). The log lists the applied toggles. Test one at a time: with several on, the wallet's first failing check wins.

Raw request
